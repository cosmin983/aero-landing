# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: a static site (plain HTML, CSS and JS on the taste-skill kit), GSAP + ScrollTrigger and Lenis installed from npm and vendored into `assets/vendor/`. No build step. Deployed to Vercel from a public GitHub repo. Chosen because a one-page landing page needs no framework, and a static folder deploys anywhere.

## Users

Remote and home-office workers who sit at a desk eight or more hours a day. Their job: get through long focus sessions without eye strain, and have the desk light stop being something they fiddle with. They buy considered objects for a workspace they look at all day.

## Product Purpose

Aero is a smart desk lamp that shifts its colour temperature and brightness through the day on its own (cool, bright light in the morning; warm, dim light in the evening) and can be controlled from a phone app. The landing page exists to get pre-orders.

## Positioning

The lamp follows the time of day automatically, so the user never has to set it. The phone is for overrides, not for daily operation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Automatic daylight-following colour temperature and brightness.
- Phone app control (iOS and Android): scenes, schedules, manual override.
- Pre-order only; nothing ships yet.
- Aero is a **fictional concept product**. Every spec, price and quote on the site is illustrative, and the site says so in its footer.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Aero.
- Pricing tiers (user-confirmed range): about $149 / $199 / $279. Lamp, lamp + wireless charging base, two-lamp bundle.

## Evidence on Hand

None. No real photographs, customers, reviews, press or test data exist. The user asked for testimonials, so they will be written as clearly illustrative pre-production tester quotes on a concept site, never presented as real customers.

## Product Principles

1. Set-and-forget beats configurable: lead with what happens automatically.
2. Calm over spectacle: it's an object for long, quiet work.
3. Honest about being a concept: no invented claims passed off as verified.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: text contrast, visible focus, keyboard reachable, respects reduced motion. Must work on phones down to 320px.
