Aero shifts from cool morning light to warm evening light on its own, timed to your sunset. From $149.

Aero follows your working day. You stop adjusting the light.

Aero runs bright and cool in the morning

Up to 1,000 lumens at 5,000 K while you read, write and take calls.

Aero turns warm and low after sunset

From late afternoon it eases toward 2,700 K and dims, so your desk is not the brightest thing in the room at 9 pm.

The base dial covers the rest

Turn the dial on the base to dim, press it to switch modes. The app remembers what you changed.

How Aero works. Three steps, about five minutes.

  1. 1

    Put Aero on your desk

    Plug in the USB-C cable. Aero starts in daylight mode straight away, with or without the app.

  2. 2

    Pair Aero with the app

    Open the Aero app on iOS or Android and tap the lamp. It reads your location once for local sunrise and sunset.

When does Aero ship?

Pre-orders ship in the order they came in. We email you a shipping date before we charge anything.

Does Aero need the app?

No. Aero follows a standard day out of the box. The app adds your exact sunrise and sunset, schedules and scenes.

Can I cancel an Aero pre-order?

Yes, at any time before it ships, from the link in your confirmation email.

Will Aero flicker on video calls?

No. The LEDs run without PWM flicker, so cameras and screen recordings stay steady.

What if the Aero app is retired?

Aero keeps its daily schedule and the dial without a phone. The app is for changes, not for running the lamp.
